Five GU RoboSphere teams selected in RoboFest Gujarat 6.0, winning Rs. 2.25 lakh.
GU ROBOSPHERE
5 GU teams selected in RoboFest Gujarat 6.0
Total prize money of ₹2.25 lakh at Level 1
Dear GU Family,
We are happy to share that five GU RoboSphere teams have been selected at Level 1 of RoboFest Gujarat 6.0, organised by Gujarat Council on Science and Technology (GUJCOST), Department of Science & Technology (DST), Government of Gujarat. Together, the teams have won a total of ₹2.25 lakh in prize money and will now move on to Level 2.
RoboFest runs two competitions
RoboFest Gujarat holds two separate competitions every year:
COMPETITION 1
Hackathon
Finished in October. Our team won the Silver Medal.
COMPETITION 2
Robotics Competition
Three levels. 5 teams cleared Level 1; Level 2 and the Grand Finale are next.
Our journey till now
 
September 2025
RoboSphere is launched
GU RoboSphere, the official robotics club of Gandhinagar University, was launched with 108 student members.
 
March 2026
Two teams in the RoboFest 5.0 Grand Finale
Two of our teams reached the Grand Finale at Gujarat Science City, Ahmedabad.
🏆 Prize money: ₹2.5 lakh
 
October 2026
Silver Medal at the RoboFest 6.0 Hackathon
Our fire-fighting robot, which detects fire through a CCTV camera, reaches the spot and sprays water, won the Silver Medal (2nd Prize) at the Grand Finale in Bhavnagar.
Mithil T Sanghani, Dhruv Pankaj Lade, Mitra Thaker and Shaurya Goswami · Mentor: Dr. Ashish Majithiya
🏆 Prize money: ₹7.5 lakh
The robot will be installed this week at the Regional Science Centre, Bhavnagar, for public exhibition.

🏆 Prize money: ₹2.25 lakh (total)
